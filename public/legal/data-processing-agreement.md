# Data Processing Agreement

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2025

## 1. Parties

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) governs the sharing and processing of personal data among the following parties:

**Data Controller:**  
City Government of Palayan, Nueva Ecija  
Acting through the Information and Communications Technology Division

**Data Processors and Partner Agencies:**
- City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)
- Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Palayan City
- City Agriculture Office
- Palayan City District Hospital
- Other authorized government departments and emergency response agencies

This DPA is executed pursuant to Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and implementing regulations, and in accordance with the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act (RA 10121).

## 2. Purpose and Scope

This agreement governs the processing, sharing, and protection of personal data collected and maintained through the CLIMA (Climate and Disaster Information Monitoring Application) platform. The purpose of data processing is to facilitate coordinated disaster response, emergency services, damage assessment, and resource allocation among partner agencies.

### Data Covered by This Agreement

- Citizen-submitted disaster and incident reports
- Personal information of reporting residents (name, contact details, location)
- Photographic evidence and documentation of disasters
- Resource status and availability data from partner agencies
- Damage assessments and impact reports
- Operational logs and response coordination data

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

Data processing under this agreement is based on the following legal grounds:

- **Legal Obligation:** Compliance with government disaster response mandates under RA 10121 (National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act)
- **Public Interest:** Performance of disaster risk reduction, emergency response, and public safety functions mandated to local government units
- **Legitimate Interests:** Coordination of emergency services for the protection of life, property, and public welfare

## 4. Data Processing Activities

Partner agencies are authorized to process shared data for the following specific purposes only:

### CDRRMO (City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office)
- Receive and assess all disaster and incident reports
- Coordinate multi-agency emergency response
- Maintain comprehensive disaster event logs
- Generate damage assessment and situation reports
- Track response progress and resource deployment

### Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)
- Access fire-related incident reports
- Coordinate fire suppression and rescue operations
- Maintain fire incident records
- Report fire response activities and outcomes

### City Agriculture Office
- Access agricultural damage reports
- Assess crop losses and farm infrastructure damage
- Coordinate agricultural recovery assistance
- Maintain agricultural disaster impact records

### Palayan City District Hospital
- Access health-related emergency reports
- Coordinate medical response and patient transport
- Track medical resource availability
- Report public health impacts of disasters

## 5. Data Subject Rights

All partner agencies must respect and facilitate the exercise of data subject rights under RA 10173:

- **Right to Access:** Data subjects may request access to their personal data processed by partner agencies
- **Right to Correction:** Data subjects may request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Data subjects may request deletion of personal data, subject to government record retention requirements
- **Right to Object:** Data subjects may object to processing beyond necessary emergency response purposes

Requests from data subjects should be forwarded to the City Government of Palayan Data Protection Officer within 5 business days.

## 6. Security Obligations

All partner agencies agree to implement and maintain appropriate security measures:

### Technical Measures
- Use secure authentication when accessing CLIMA platform
- Access data only through authorized government accounts
- Employ encryption for data in transit and at rest
- Implement password policies and multi-factor authentication where available
- Maintain updated antivirus and security software on devices accessing CLIMA data

### Organizational Measures
- Limit data access to authorized personnel with legitimate need-to-know
- Provide data protection training to personnel handling personal data
- Maintain audit logs of data access and processing activities
- Implement clean desk and secure document disposal policies
- Execute confidentiality agreements with personnel handling personal data

### Access Control
- Designate authorized users and maintain updated access lists
- Immediately revoke access when personnel leave or change roles
- Report unauthorized access attempts to the Data Protection Officer
- Use role-based access controls to limit data visibility

## 7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, the affected partner agency must:

1. **Immediate Notification:** Notify the City Government of Palayan Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery
2. **Incident Documentation:** Document the nature, scope, and timeline of the breach
3. **Impact Assessment:** Assess the number of affected data subjects and types of personal data compromised
4. **Containment Measures:** Take immediate steps to contain and mitigate the breach
5. **Cooperation:** Cooperate fully in breach investigation and remediation efforts
6. **Regulatory Reporting:** Assist in reporting to the National Privacy Commission as required by law

The City Government of Palayan will notify affected data subjects in accordance with RA 10173 requirements.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

### Retention Periods
- **Active Incident Reports:** Retained for duration of emergency response and recovery operations
- **Closed Incident Reports:** Retained permanently as government disaster response records per National Archives guidelines
- **Operational Logs:** Retained for 5 years for audit and analysis purposes
- **Personal Contact Data:** Retained while necessary for follow-up and verification; anonymized after 2 years if no longer needed

### Secure Deletion
When data retention periods expire, partner agencies must securely delete or anonymize personal data using methods that prevent recovery, including secure file deletion and physical destruction of paper records.

## 9. Sub-Processing

The City Government of Palayan engages the following sub-processors for technical infrastructure:

**Supabase (Database and Authentication Services)**  
Purpose: Data storage, user authentication, file hosting  
Location: Cloud infrastructure with data encryption  
Safeguards: Data Processing Agreement, encryption, access controls

**Mapbox (Mapping Services)**  
Purpose: Geographic mapping and location services  
Data Shared: Location coordinates only, no personally identifiable information  
Safeguards: API key authentication, limited data access

Partner agencies must not engage additional sub-processors or third-party service providers for processing CLIMA data without prior written approval from the Data Protection Officer.

## 10. Data Transfer Restrictions

Partner agencies may not:

- Transfer personal data to third parties outside the authorized partner agencies
- Use CLIMA data for purposes other than disaster response coordination
- Combine CLIMA data with external databases without authorization
- Disclose personal data publicly or to media without anonymization
- Export bulk personal data from CLIMA platform without written authorization

Emergency disclosures to other government agencies during active disaster response are permitted when necessary to protect life and property.

## 11. Audit and Compliance

The City Government of Palayan reserves the right to:

- Audit partner agency data processing practices upon reasonable notice
- Request documentation of security measures and access controls
- Review access logs and processing activities
- Conduct data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing

Partner agencies agree to cooperate fully with audits and provide requested documentation within 15 business days.

## 12. Liability and Indemnification

Each partner agency is responsible for:

- Damages resulting from unauthorized disclosure or misuse of personal data under their control
- Costs of breach notification and remediation resulting from their security failures
- Regulatory fines and penalties resulting from their non-compliance with RA 10173

Agencies agree to indemnify the City Government of Palayan against third-party claims arising from their data processing violations.

## 13. Training and Awareness

Partner agencies must provide annual data protection training to all personnel with access to CLIMA data, covering:

- Data Privacy Act requirements and penalties
- CLIMA data handling procedures
- Security best practices
- Incident reporting procedures
- Data subject rights

## 14. Term and Termination

This agreement remains in effect while partner agencies have authorized access to CLIMA platform. 

### Termination
Either party may terminate participation in CLIMA with 30 days written notice to the Data Protection Officer.

### Upon Termination
- Access credentials will be revoked immediately
- Partner agency must securely delete or return all personal data extracts
- Historical disaster response records remain subject to government retention requirements
- Confidentiality obligations survive termination indefinitely

## 15. Amendments

This Data Processing Agreement may be amended to reflect changes in legal requirements, data protection best practices, or operational needs. Partner agencies will be notified of material changes with 30 days advance notice. Continued use of CLIMA after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 16. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, including Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act), and implementing regulations issued by the National Privacy Commission.

## 17. Dispute Resolution

Disputes arising from this agreement will be resolved through consultation among partner agencies and the City Government of Palayan. Unresolved disputes may be escalated to the Office of the City Mayor for administrative resolution.
