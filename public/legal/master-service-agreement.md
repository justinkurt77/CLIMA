# Master Service Agreement

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2025

## 1. Parties to the Agreement

This Master Service Agreement (MSA) establishes the terms and conditions for the use of the CLIMA (Climate and Disaster Information Monitoring Application) platform among the following parties:

**Platform Provider:**  
City Government of Palayan, Nueva Ecija  
Acting through the Information and Communications Technology Division

**Participating Agencies:**
- City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)
- Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Palayan City
- City Agriculture Office
- Palayan City District Hospital
- City Mayor's Office
- City Planning and Development Office
- Other authorized city government departments

This agreement governs the operational, technical, and administrative aspects of CLIMA platform usage for disaster response coordination.

## 2. Purpose and Scope

CLIMA is a shared disaster response and climate monitoring platform designed to facilitate:

- Real-time disaster and incident reporting by Palayan City residents
- Coordinated emergency response among government agencies
- Resource tracking and damage assessment
- Weather advisory distribution
- Historical disaster data management for preparedness planning

This MSA defines service levels, data governance, operational responsibilities, and coordination protocols among participating agencies.

## 3. Service Description

### Platform Features and Capabilities

**For Citizens:**
- Web-based disaster and incident reporting with geolocation
- Photo upload and evidence documentation
- Report status tracking and updates
- Weather advisory notifications
- Emergency contact information access

**For Government Agencies:**
- Administrative dashboard with report management
- Department-specific report routing and filtering
- Status update and response coordination tools
- Mapping interface with incident visualization
- Export and reporting capabilities for damage assessment

### Technical Infrastructure
- Cloud-based platform hosted on Supabase infrastructure
- Mapbox integration for geolocation and mapping services
- Mobile-responsive web interface accessible from any device
- Secure authentication and role-based access control

## 4. Service Level Expectations

While CLIMA is provided on a best-effort basis as government infrastructure, the City Government of Palayan strives to maintain the following service levels:

### Availability
- **Target Uptime:** 99% monthly uptime (excluding scheduled maintenance)
- **Scheduled Maintenance Windows:** Monthly maintenance on the second Sunday from 2:00 AM to 6:00 AM
- **Emergency Maintenance:** As needed with advance notice when possible

### Performance
- **Page Load Time:** Target of under 3 seconds for dashboard and report pages
- **Report Submission:** Target processing time of under 5 seconds
- **Map Rendering:** Target initial map load of under 5 seconds

### Disaster Response Priority
During active disaster situations, CLIMA platform may experience increased load. The ICT Division will prioritize platform stability and emergency response functions over non-critical features.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

### City Government of Palayan (ICT Division)

**Platform Operation:**
- Maintain and operate CLIMA infrastructure
- Provide technical support to participating agencies
- Implement security updates and patches
- Monitor system performance and uptime
- Manage user accounts and access control

**Data Protection:**
- Serve as data controller for personal information
- Ensure compliance with Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act)
- Implement security measures and access controls
- Respond to data subject rights requests

**Communication:**
- Notify agencies of scheduled maintenance at least 72 hours in advance
- Communicate service disruptions within 1 hour of detection
- Provide monthly platform usage and performance reports

### Participating Agencies

**Operational Responsibilities:**
- Monitor and respond to disaster reports within assigned categories
- Update report status as response actions are taken
- Maintain accurate resource and status information
- Participate in training and onboarding for new personnel
- Report technical issues and platform feedback to ICT Division

**Data Responsibilities:**
- Process personal data only for authorized disaster response purposes
- Comply with Data Processing Agreement terms
- Protect authentication credentials and prevent unauthorized access
- Report security incidents to Data Protection Officer within 24 hours

**Coordination:**
- Participate in quarterly CLIMA coordination meetings
- Provide input on platform improvements and feature requests
- Share lessons learned and best practices for disaster response

## 6. Data Governance

### Data Ownership
All disaster reports and associated data collected through CLIMA are owned by the City Government of Palayan as official government records. Participating agencies are granted access to this data for operational purposes under the Data Processing Agreement.

### Data Quality
Agencies are responsible for:
- Verifying accuracy of reports within their domain before taking action
- Updating status information accurately and in a timely manner
- Reporting duplicate or fraudulent reports
- Maintaining data integrity in damage assessments and resource tracking

### Data Retention
- Active incident reports: Retained indefinitely as government disaster response records
- Historical reports: Maintained for analysis, planning, and compliance purposes
- User accounts: Subject to Privacy Policy retention schedules

## 7. Security and Access Control

### Account Management
- Each agency designates an Agency Coordinator responsible for requesting user accounts
- User access is granted based on legitimate operational need
- Accounts are role-based: Superadmin, Department Admin, or Standard User
- Access is revoked immediately upon personnel transfer or separation

### Security Requirements
- All users must use strong passwords (minimum 8 characters, complexity requirements)
- Accounts must not be shared among multiple individuals
- Access is logged and subject to audit
- Suspicious activity must be reported immediately

### Incident Reporting
Agencies must report the following to ict@palayancity.gov.ph:
- Compromised user credentials
- Suspected unauthorized access
- Lost or stolen devices with CLIMA access
- Data breaches or security incidents

## 8. Training and Support

### Initial Onboarding
The ICT Division provides onboarding training for new agency users covering:
- Platform navigation and core features
- Report management workflows
- Data privacy and security obligations
- Incident reporting procedures

### Ongoing Support
- **Technical Support:** Available via email (ict@palayancity.gov.ph) and phone (044-940-1234) during business hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday)
- **Emergency Support:** Available 24/7 during active disaster response operations
- **Documentation:** User guides and training materials accessible through CLIMA platform

### Quarterly Coordination Meetings
Participating agencies convene quarterly to:
- Review platform usage and response metrics
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Share operational feedback
- Coordinate on upcoming features and changes

## 9. Incident Response Coordination

### Report Routing
- Citizens submit reports through CLIMA web interface
- Reports are automatically visible to CDRRMO (central coordination)
- Reports are routed to specialized departments based on category (Fire, Agriculture, Health)
- Agencies receive notifications for reports in their categories

### Response Workflow
1. **Receipt:** Agency receives report notification
2. **Assessment:** Agency personnel assess report validity and urgency
3. **Dispatch:** Emergency response is coordinated as appropriate
4. **Status Update:** Agency updates report status (Acknowledged, In Progress, Resolved)
5. **Documentation:** Agency documents response actions and outcomes

### Multi-Agency Coordination
For incidents requiring multiple agencies, CDRRMO serves as the primary coordinator using CLIMA as the shared situational awareness platform.

## 10. Service Modifications

The City Government of Palayan reserves the right to:

- Modify platform features and functionality
- Add or remove participating agencies
- Update security requirements and access policies
- Adjust service level expectations based on resource availability

Participating agencies will be notified of material changes at least 30 days in advance when feasible.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

CLIMA is provided as government infrastructure to support disaster response coordination. The City Government of Palayan is not liable for:

- Damages resulting from service interruptions or system failures
- Losses arising from delayed emergency response
- Consequences of inaccurate or fraudulent citizen reports
- Technical issues with third-party services (Supabase, Mapbox)

This limitation applies to the fullest extent permitted under Philippine law.

## 12. Dispute Resolution

Operational disputes or disagreements regarding CLIMA usage among participating agencies will be resolved through the following escalation process:

1. **Agency Coordinators:** Initial discussion between affected agencies
2. **CDRRMO Chief:** Escalation to CDRRMO as central disaster coordinator
3. **City Mayor's Office:** Final administrative resolution by the Office of the City Mayor

## 13. Termination and Withdrawal

### Agency Withdrawal
A participating agency may withdraw from CLIMA usage by providing 30 days written notice to the ICT Division. Upon withdrawal:
- User accounts are deactivated
- Access to historical data may be retained for archival purposes
- Agency remains obligated to protect data confidentiality

### Service Termination
The City Government of Palayan may terminate an agency's access for:
- Material breach of security or data protection obligations
- Repeated policy violations
- Abuse of platform privileges

Termination does not affect historical disaster response records.

## 14. Amendment Process

This Master Service Agreement may be amended through the following process:

1. **Proposal:** ICT Division proposes amendment with rationale
2. **Review:** Participating agencies review and provide feedback (30-day comment period)
3. **Coordination Meeting:** Amendment discussed at quarterly coordination meeting
4. **Approval:** Final amendment approved by City Mayor or designated authority
5. **Notification:** Agencies notified of approved amendments with effective date

## 15. Compliance and Audit

Participating agencies agree to:

- Comply with all CLIMA policies, including Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement
- Participate in periodic compliance audits
- Provide documentation of security measures upon request
- Cooperate with investigations of policy violations or security incidents

## 16. Governing Law

This Master Service Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines and administrative regulations applicable to local government units.

## 17. Entire Agreement

This MSA, together with the Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement among participating agencies regarding CLIMA platform usage and supersedes all prior understandings or agreements.

## 18. Severability
