## 2. Service Description

CLIMA is a free government service provided by the City Government of Palayan to support disaster preparedness, response, and climate monitoring for the residents of Palayan City and surrounding areas in Nueva Ecija, Philippines. The platform enables:

- Real-time disaster and incident reporting by residents
- Geolocation-based mapping of disaster events and hazards
- Coordination between government agencies including the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO), Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), City Agriculture Office, and Palayan City District Hospital
- Distribution of weather advisories, warnings, and official announcements
- Tracking and status updates for reported incidents
- Emergency contact information and response resources

This is a free public service. There are no subscription fees, usage charges, or premium tiers.
