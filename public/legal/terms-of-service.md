Welcome to CLIMA (Climate Information Monitoring and Awareness), the official disaster response and climate monitoring platform of Palayan City, Nueva Ecija, Philippines. CLIMA is operated by the Palayan City Information and Communications Technology (ICT) Office in coordination with the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO), Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Department of Health (DOH), and other government agencies.

By accessing or using CLIMA, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree to these terms, please do not use the platform.

## 2. Service Description

CLIMA provides the following services to residents and stakeholders of Palayan City:

- **Real-time Weather Monitoring:** Temperature, humidity, heat index, wind speed, and weather forecasts
- **Disaster Reporting:** Citizen-submitted incident reports with photo documentation and GPS coordinates
- **Emergency Advisories:** Official alerts and bulletins from city government agencies
- **Resource Tracking:** Hospital bed capacity, emergency operations status, and utility service interruptions
- **Agricultural Damage Monitoring:** Crop damage assessments and farmer support coordination
- **Public Dashboard:** Aggregated statistics and trends for transparency and situational awareness
